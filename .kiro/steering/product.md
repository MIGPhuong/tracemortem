# Product steering — TraceMortem

- Target audience: small developer teams and on-call engineers.
- Promise: evidence visibility, not autonomous root-cause certainty.
- Every suspicious claim must link to real ingested event IDs.
- UI should look like a detective's evidence board without compromising practical usability.
- No sign-up wall, SaaS billing flow, or analytics in the hackathon MVP.
- Keep sample cases synthetic and prominently labeled.
- Never conflate imported sample errors with global production error rates.
